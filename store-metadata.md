Transform your financial landscape from simple tracking to strategic intelligence. KONTROLA is a professional-grade command center designed for individuals and businesses who demand high-fidelity clarity over their cash flow, assets, and obligations.

- **Refuel Predictor**: Proactive analytics that estimate "Days Until Refuel" based on your unique movement patterns.

- **Outflow Visibility**: Immediate clarity on maintenance costs, subscriptions, and recurring debts.

**AI ADVISOR**
- **Strategic Insights**: Personalized, data-driven advice to optimize your savings and reduce unnecessary outflow.
- **Financial Maturity Score**: A proprietary indicator of your overall financial health and liquidity.
